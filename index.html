Fishy Shooter

Controls:

  • W or Up Arrow: Move forward.
  • S or Down Arrow: Move backward.
  • A or Left Arrow: Turn left.
  • D or Right Arrow: Turn right.
  • Left click: Shoot..
    • Acid Bubbles: Shoots Bubbles forward. Hold to shoot continuosly.
    • Pressure Stream: Shoots a long distance stream. Halts movement while shooting.
    • Bite: Lunges forward slightly. Restores health on kill.
  • Middle Mouse or Space: Movement Skill.
    • Flip Turn: Turns the fish around 180 degrees.
    • Dash: Moves forward a set distance.
    • Roll: Moves the fish to the right or left.
